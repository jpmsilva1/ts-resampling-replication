NOTE ON SIGN: tau = mean(log(SERA_strategy) - log(SERA_baseline)). SERA is
lower-is-better, so a POSITIVE tau means the strategy is WORSE than baseline
(matches generate_regression.py's coefficient sign -- e.g. SMOTEB here should
equal that script's SMOTEB coefficient exactly). This is the OPPOSITE sign
convention from the Bayes charts' ROPE, which is defined as
log(baseline) - log(strategy) (positive = strategy better) -- don't compare
signs across the two without checking which convention each uses.
