=== F1: pooled causal effect (tau_k vs. baseline) ===
Randomization inference, permutation within (dataset, model_family) blocks,
N_perm=10000. Holm-adjusted p controls the family-wise error across
these 9 strategies (confirmatory). Point estimate = mean paired difference
across all 120 blocks (24 datasets); identical to the two-way FE OLS
coefficient under
this design's full balance.
